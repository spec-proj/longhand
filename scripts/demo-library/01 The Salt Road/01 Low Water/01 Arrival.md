# Arrival

She walked out at low tide and counted the ribs of a wreck. The sand was *cold* under her feet, and the light came in sideways from the estuary, the colour of weak tea.

Her brother had written that the house would be empty. It was not. A lamp burned in the upper window, and somebody had swept the step.

> Come when the water is out, he had said. Not before.

She set down the case and listened. Gulls, the far hiss of the channel, and under it — **very faintly** — a piano.
